About StudyTRACK

Why was StudyTRACK created?

Many students have difficulty organizing their study tasks and keeping track of their progress.
StudyTRACK was created to provide a simple way to organize studying.

Our Goal

The goal of StudyTRACK is to make study planning simple, clear and easy to manage.

What can students do with StudyTRACK?

  • Organize their Subjects
  • Manage their study Tasks
  • Follow their Progress
